Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Ceitus ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you visit our website, purchase our licenses, or interact with our Discord bots and services.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to fulfill our services and provide license authentication:

  • Account Identifiers: Your Discord User ID, username, and email address provided during checkout or authentication.
  • Transaction Data: Transaction ID, plan purchased, license status, and payment timestamp (transmitted securely via Paddle webhooks). We do not store full credit card numbers or banking secrets on our servers.
  • Technical Logs: Anonymized bot usage logs, error reports, and hardware profile hashes strictly for anti-abuse and license verification.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party providers to deliver our operations:

  • Paddle.com: Acts as our Merchant of Record. Payments, billing details, VAT/tax handling, and credit card processing are conducted securely under Paddle's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: Provides secure cloud database storage with row-level security for role mappings and license validity states.

4. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive communications are encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS 1.3 cryptographic protocols. Access to cloud databases is restricted by least-privilege service credentials and protected by rate-limiting defenses.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your account records. To exercise any of these rights, contact our privacy team.
