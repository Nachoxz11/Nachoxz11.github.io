Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 | Last Updated: November 1, 2026

1. Merchant of Record

All orders are processed and billed by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. Inquiries regarding invoices, payment receipts, charge questions, or official refunds are handled directly by Paddle and Ceitus support.

2. 14-Day Refund Guarantee

We want you to be fully satisfied with our Discord automation and utility software. We offer a full 14-day refund window from the date of purchase under the following circumstances:

  • The digital license key has not been redeemed or permanently bound to a machine/guild.
  • You experience an irreconcilable technical issue with our software or bot service that our support team cannot resolve within 72 hours of report.
  • You made an accidental duplicate payment for the same plan.

3. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel recurring subscriptions at any time with zero cancellation penalties. You may execute cancellation via:

  • The customer self-service link included in your original Paddle email receipt.
  • Opening a support ticket in our Discord community.
  • Sending an email to support@ceitus.com with your order number.

Once cancelled, your plan will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle, and no further charges will occur.

4. Exceptions and Abuse

Refunds will not be granted in cases where terms of service have been violated, such as unauthorized resale of keys, credential sharing, chargeback fraud, or attempted security tampering.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please send an email to support@ceitus.com or contact Paddle buyer support at paddle.net with your:

  1. Paddle Order Number / Transaction ID.
  2. Email address used during checkout.
  3. Brief explanation of the issue encountered.

Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method (Credit card, PayPal) typically within 3 to 5 business days depending on your bank.
